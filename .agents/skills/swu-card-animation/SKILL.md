# SWU card animation

Make the card's action readable on the table: who acted, what was affected, what changed, and where it went. An effect is complete only when the ordinary board still shows its result after the animation disappears.

Work from the `swu_bot` repository root. Read `CLAUDE.md`, [the visual language](../../../docs/card-animation-language.md), [the batch ledger](../../../docs/card-animation-batches.md), and relevant entries in [the reuse catalogue](../../../docs/card-animation-reuse.md). Use installed card JSON under `forceteki/test/json/Card/` and the matching implementation under `forceteki/server/game/cards/` as read-only rules sources. Do not modify the engine or infer mechanics from a card's title/art.

## Scope and batch contract

- Resolve the requested catalogue scope from the current user instructions and ledger. Imported identities, print variants, implemented bot-index cards, and animation examples are different counts. Reprints share a scene only after verifying equivalent rules and registering their exact print identities.
- Work in rounds of ten identities, with the final remainder allowed. Start at `xhigh` reasoning on the requested GPT model. Record the actual model/effort used; clarify an ambiguous model name or state the working interpretation before dispatch.
- Preserve the approved twenty compositions and timing signatures while repairing state handoff. New cards may reuse their mechanical primitives, but need choreography appropriate to their own action. A shared glow with a new title is not a finished scene.
- Maintain one writer per shared integration file. Give authoring/review agents exact cards, owned files, current skill, rules sources, and acceptance evidence. Independent review must inspect the resulting pixels and state, not repeat the author's claims.

## Author a card

1. **Write a compact effect specification.** Record canonical identity/prints, every legal clause, target restrictions, chosen modes, amount, owner/controller, destination, condition, duration, and optional actions. Distinguish complete rules from the one staged example. A replay only depicts outcomes supported by recorded evidence.
2. **Build a legal example with explicit before/after snapshots.** Give every card instance a stable ID. Stage actual prerequisite units/traits, sufficient resources, existing damage for healing, correct upgrade hosts, and real source cards for movement. Explain selected modes and satisfied conditions in the example label. Do not claim a hidden prerequisite occurred without staging or documenting it.
   Verify the card name against its print ID and stats. Pay the printed cost, missing-aspect penalties (including repeated symbols), and chosen extra payments exactly once; an extra-payment badge does not account for the play cost. Phase statuses such as suppression must remain visible on the completed board as well as changing numbers.
   A rich animation's evidence predicate must compare declared payment with the independently computed legal cost and canonical source identity. A08's five secondary predicates accepted internally consistent underpayments until independent review varied the cost, paid IDs and ending together. Probe those coherent contradictions, plus explicitly prevented receipts behind otherwise successful endings; checking only resource totals or the final board is insufficient.
   Inspect those statuses in actual completed-board pixels: a label present in the DOM can still be clipped by the card's artwork mask. Keep the status within visible bounds without covering the stat counters.
3. **Describe anticipation → action → held result.** Use readable motion, shape, destination, and numbers; color reinforces these. Anchor to the measured card/zone, and keep important action clear of captions. Scale spectacle to the effect: Repair's focused grid and Superlaser Blast's six-target sweep both work because their scope is unmistakable.
4. **Implement through the existing seams.** `catalogue.ts` holds identity and timing; `abilities.ts` holds complete clauses and example choices; `showcase.ts`/`preview.ts` provide staged evidence; `detect.ts`/`outcomes.ts` extract replay evidence; `CardEffectOverlay.tsx`, `primitives.tsx`, and `animationTimeline.ts` render it. Keep one deterministic Anime.js clock for live playback and scrubbing. Add a shared primitive only for a new mechanical meaning, and a card signature for its distinct choreography.

All paths above are under `studio/src/effects/`. Inspect current code before editing; these are responsibilities, not a requirement to change every file.

## Presentation state is part of the effect

`CardEffectCue.transition` carries explicit `before` and `after` snapshots. For a recorded replay, both come from the recording: do not reconstruct final game truth by applying visual outcome badges or card rules. For a staged example, author a deliberate result separate from actual match evidence.

- Render departing cards from the before snapshot while their motion needs them. On completion, hand off to the after snapshot and remove the overlay. Defeated/returned cards must not reappear; damage, healing, tokens, exhaustion, ownership, resources, hand/deck/discard counts must persist as applicable.
- Make completion idempotent. Replaying starts from the same before state; backward seeking restores that state; selecting another card or replay step cancels the old transition. A stale completion callback must never overwrite the new selection. Measure a new cue only after its board commits; initialize geometry from an inexact fallback, not the preceding DOM. A05 Spark of Hope inherited an exact arena coordinate from Palpatine’s Return and highlighted a surviving unit instead of discard. Review entering a scene from another scene as well as Replay within it.
- Generated example cards and tokens need stable identities across replay. A playback nonce identifies the clock; using it in card IDs made Force Choke's natural ending differ from its held ending despite identical pixels. A newly created token also needs a physical ID absent from the complete prior-incarnation history. A15 Scramble and Wings accepted reused physical IDs with different incarnations until coherent history probes exposed the gap; changing the incarnation does not make an old token ID new.
- A frozen 90% frame still contains an overlay. It cannot prove completed-board behavior. Test natural playback completion and an explicit completed state, then reset/replay without cumulative changes.
- Keep visible source disposal consistent with payment during the animation too. A06's paid events stayed in the old hand until completion even though their final discard was correct; hide or route that paid source at its actual resolution beat. Continuing survivors moving into rearranged final slots must keep their opacity: applying a new-arrival fade made Headhunting's survivors briefly disappear during layout settlement.
- The showcase exposes **Final board** at 100%, and the board reports `data-effect-state="settled"`. Use these for deterministic inspection, but also observe natural completion; a scrubbed final image cannot prove the completion callback works.
- Preserve unknowns in recordings. A disappeared card with no recorded destination is not necessarily defeated. Minimal discard references may omit final damage. Suppression, inspection, discounts, and extra payments need explicit evidence to claim their details. Identity matching must not guess between duplicate copies or fall back from an unknown print ID to a title.
- Use the same evidence predicate to render a rich signature and suppress the generic receipts it replaces. Bamboozle's sparse recorded upgrade returns could otherwise disappear when its host was already exhausted and no new exhaustion receipt existed. Unsupported choice, payment, chronology or token-cause details must remain unclaimed while supported movement still renders.
- Validate compound receipts against each other and their source/before/after snapshots. A04 caught absent payment displayed as zero and unrelated hits grouped into a sequence. A06 caught a different causing event, invented current attack power, declared rescue exhaustion contradicted by the actual ending, and random selection shown as player choice. Known zero needs an explicit receipt; ordered hits need matching sequence/target and distinct sources when required. Explicit contradictory metadata cannot be ignored merely because the usual primary example is valid. Require the exact supported success result; treating every value except the failure literal as success allowed missing/unknown Political Pressure results through A13. A11 secondary signatures accepted unreceipted initiative changes and unrelated success, trait or attack constraints behind otherwise correct endings. Bind the unchanged turn state and reject an explicit constraint that the signature has not established. A12 added coherent regroup plays and invented ending permissions/pending defeats: validate the legal play phase and all otherwise unchanged snapshot metadata, not only matching round/phase and visible card zones. A17 coherent probes also require a still-active match, a live base, no incompatible current next-action obligation, and current entries consistent with recorded incarnation history. Comparing two equally invalid snapshots is not a legal-state check; normalizing minimal zone references must not discard an unreceipted body or status added to a hand card.

## Mechanical distinctions that must survive the art

- Damage increases damage markers; defeat removes an object; healing removes existing damage without increasing maximum HP; debuffs change stats without inventing damage. A11's surviving printed-Grit units exposed a second dependency: receiving wounds can change resting power. A rich damage/attack signature must establish that change or leave the variant generic; cloning old power with new wounds is not sufficient evidence.
- Return moves an intact unit to its owner's hand. Discard starts in hand; mill starts at deck top; recovery starts in discard. Show the actual destination and count. Removing an upgrade must not remove its host unless a separately supported consequence does so.
- Phase buffs and Experience tokens are different. Attack-only bonuses expire after the attack. Delayed regroup consequences must not be shown as immediate removals or permanent ownership changes.
- For an authored delayed effect, provide separately selectable immediate and trigger-boundary scenes. The ordinary play must retain the pending result; the boundary must resolve it without replaying costs, drawing/readying early, or acting on a later incarnation of a departed card. Never let elapsed animation time trigger future game rules.
- When a signature already explains a compound result, avoid duplicating its full clause in a narrow generic badge. Upgrade removal must hide the measured attachment badge at its removal beat without covering the surviving host. Use the existing `AttachmentMask` for chips: A08 Confiscate reused a whole-card mask and clipped the retained Shield edge. Keep the travelling upgrade face in card proportions even when it starts at a wide, shallow chip. Precision Fire's symbolic Shield fracture left the original Shield badge visible during combat; a correct final board did not repair that contradiction. Measure arriving cards against the final arena layout, including mobile wrapping; an approximate open slot can overlap an existing unit and jump on handoff. Check shrinking lanes too: A12 Clear the Field measured an earlier final hand row while the visible before board still held its old ground rows, landing a returned card over a survivor. Keep clearance calculations invariant under scrolling: A12 treated an absent optional shelf as viewport coordinate0, producing a huge footer when the board was above the viewport. Use only present measured bounds. Include explicit resource racks in layout stability: A05’s 20→17 resource-pip wrap moved the headquarters and arena landings by 16px. Keep the actual before/after footprints stable or measure their distinct layouts.
- Clamp a receipt stack as a group near the caption boundary. Individually clamping three labels folded the ownership/payment row under the delayed-result row on mobile, despite distinct intended coordinates. Size the box for its actual longest text too: A11's five secondary receipts clipped leading numbers and final letters inside an otherwise correctly placed box. Inspect both ends of the primary label in pixels at desktop and mobile widths.
- Check measured health numerals along card routes, including between fixed capture beats. A08's captive discard and selected-search flights obscured mobile base HP near 48% despite correct endpoints. A detour must also clear neighboring unit counters; use a smaller reveal while crossing a narrow gap and grow it only in clear space when needed. A10's U-Wing follow-up showed that even a 20px card can cover a desktop numeral when its final horizontal leg shares the discard icon's row. Give that leg a measured offset before approaching the destination. On wrapped mobile rows, labels above a new unit can cover the previous row's counters; keep arrival labels inside the arriving face or another verified clear area.
- Measured destination geometry must also clear the caption. Search, recovery and draw ghosts reached the correct bottom-hand slots but disappeared behind the caption on mobile; inspect the arrival objects themselves through the held result, not only the overlay-free ending. Check the departure too: A04 Galactic Ambition passed the fixed frames but its hand-origin card and counters were hidden by the caption near 30% of natural playback. Reserve space for supported hand departures and inspect the route between the named capture beats. Check the signature’s own labels too: clearing the caption exposed a second collision where FREE HAND PLAY covered the travelling Wampa’s counters.
- Modal cards show the chosen modes only. Conditional benefits require actual prerequisites. Attacks require a ready legal attacker and supported damage/counterdamage; a base does not counterattack.
- Preserve who makes a selection in the visible receipts and example. Power of the Dark Side initially showed a correct defeat but lost the opponent's choice between rules metadata and rendering; its choice attribution must be visible with the result.
- Board-wide and arena-wide effects include every eligible side and zone. State the selected arena. A three-unit sequence must use three distinct legal recipients when the rules require them.

## Verify the result, then count it

Run `npm --prefix studio test` and `npm --prefix studio run build` after implementation. Add focused state assertions for new mechanics, identity ambiguity, interrupted transitions, or lifecycle changes. Do not write tests that merely echo the declaration being tested.
Read authoritative installed JSON directly in persistent tests; ignored generated review inventories must not become setup prerequisites.
For available recorded examples, use `studio/scripts/verify-effect-replays.mjs --cards <canonical-ids>` against the matching production build. It checks actual before/after handoff, Replay and backward seek, and reports absent recordings separately; it does not generate games or claim replay coverage for missing cases.

Capture each card at 18%, 46%, 72%, and 90%, **plus an overlay-free completed-board image after natural playback**. Use the repository's existing browser dependencies and `studio/scripts/capture-effects.mjs`; inspect `--help` before running because its completed-state support and catalogue limit may change. If the tool lacks the fifth capture, add that support or capture it through the visible UI and record it explicitly. Give each batch its own output directory under `results/card-effects-review/`; preserve a manifest with card identity, example, viewport, progress, image hashes, source revision/fingerprint, and browser errors.

Assert selected title, caption, and progress before every frozen capture. Derive the expected title from the requested identity, not the current rendered title: a sampler that checked its observed title against itself missed same-document hash navigation retaining Bravado under four other filenames. Select through the UI or remount, then assert the intended identity. Wait for fonts/art and for edits to stop reloading the page. For tall mobile captures, offscreen lazy images can leave decode promises pending; make capture images eager and bound readiness waits, preserving any failed attempt. Stable pixels alone missed a prior HMR reset to the wrong card. Do not overwrite reviewed evidence with images from a different source revision under the same pass claim.
Include shared board/card styles and the showcase page in the capture fingerprint; effect-module hashes alone missed a status-label CSS correction.
Use the served build's source manifest for production captures, and keep that build frozen while reviewing; unbuilt work on the next batch is a separate revision. Park the pointer outside cards after selecting, scrolling and clicking Replay so a hover inspector cannot obscure review evidence. Inspect natural completed pixels separately: A09 exposed a stale floating zoom after its source card unmounted; pointer placement alone cannot prove the product clears stale hover state.
If a frozen-image hash differs, inspect and quantify the difference alongside repeated progress and geometry. Save both images: a hash without its comparison image cannot establish whether the difference was harmless. The capture script retains `settle-90-repeat.png` and both identity/progress and board-relative geometry measurements. A02 and A06 had tiny rounded-edge raster changes with fixed geometry; preserve that failed exact-hash evidence and the diagnosis rather than recapturing until a favorable hash appears.

An independent reviewer inspects the ordered frames and completed board, scoring **readability / meaning / impact**, each 0–5. Accept a card when readability and meaning are at least 4, impact at least 3, and there is no material contradiction, missing result, clipped primary information, or state assertion failure. A concise, clear effect can earn impact 3; more particles are not required. Record residual polish separately. Inspect live timing for new signatures and transitions; frozen frames alone cannot judge pacing.

Check 390×844 mobile layouts for new movement/rotation, wide scope, and dense compound effects. SVG `preserveAspectRatio="none"` needs aspect-compensated rotations and card dimensions; raw viewBox rotations previously turned cards into thin strips. Verify reduced-motion completion for changed playback behavior. Review affected existing scenes whenever a shared primitive changes.

Count a card complete only after its rules/example assertions, completed-board assertions, and independent visual review pass. A generated scene, build success, screenshot manifest, or past approval at 90% is not sufficient. Record the precise remaining gap when blocked; continue independent cards that do not depend on it.

## Improve the skill from measured batches

Update the ledger after every round: actual model/effort, identities, authored versus regression work, first-pass accepted count, failures, fixes, tests, capture/review locations, and remaining work. Add a narrow skill correction only for an observed failure or a demonstrated improvement; avoid accumulating speculative rules.

Maintain the reuse catalogue as repeated patterns appear. Record concrete cards, shared or duplicated helpers, minimum outcome evidence, rule variants, unknown-data fallback, tests and capture references. Distinguish existing reuse, proposed extraction and unreviewed drafts. Derive choreography from verified receipts; similar text or endpoints alone do not justify recycling a richer story. Preserve the same evidence predicate for rendering and generic-receipt suppression, and review affected accepted scenes when extracting shared code. The catalogue is preparation for a later refactor, not authorization to replace working signatures with a speculative framework.

Keep `xhigh` until **two consecutive newly authored ten-card batches pass 10/10 on their first independent review, with no semantic or state regressions**. The twenty-card baseline regression establishes handoff stability and does not qualify as new-authoring reliability. Then trial one level lower (`high`) on the next ten, using the same checks. Reduce another level only after two clean authored batches at that level. A material failure restores the last reliable higher effort, resets the clean streak, and triggers a focused refinement. The user can override this operating policy.
