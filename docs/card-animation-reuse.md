# Card animation reuse catalogue

This is the working catalogue for a later refactor, requested on 2026-10-11. It records demonstrated repetition, the evidence needed to derive a scene, and differences that must survive reuse. Add discoveries as batches reveal them; this is not an exhaustive classification of future cards.

Current evidence: **140 independently accepted identities, 150 registered**. A01–A12 are accepted. A12 final140 source9cf05 passed317 independent gates,25 source oracles and26 rendered ending/lifecycle cases; its initial18 failures across five secondary identities remain first-pass ceiling5/10, clean streak0. Root's A13 five scenes are author-clear at isolated615cd; initial complete150 source09ec5d62 passes452 tests/typecheck and four existing replays, with six recordings absent. Main author is correcting three temporary-display contradictions before independent pixels. A14 data and root proofs remain isolated; A15 has a source-backed specification and finite contracts. Unaccepted scenes do not increase the accepted count.

The useful derivation boundary is **verified outcomes → reusable choreography**. Card names, similar rules text and matching endpoint snapshots are insufficient to choose a richer sequence. Rules/recording adapters establish what happened; rendering can then recycle its motion. A single card may compose several recipes.

## Suggested refactor order

1. **Consolidate mechanical helpers (R01).** Compare variants first; keep the newer continuous travel and pose behavior. Migrate one accepted pair and review both sizes plus the affected hand/arena regressions before wider substitution.
2. **Extract private-search and owner-return presentation (R04, R09).** They have multiple demonstrated examples, different policies and useful negative cases. Keep per-card eligibility proofs while sharing the rendering plan.
3. **Extract evidence-checked entry and payment composition (R02, R05).** Represent explicit origin, intermediate hand waypoint, cost, readiness and later obligations without guessing absent facts.
4. **Generalize combat and later boundaries only from tested variants (R08, R12).** Their visible similarities conceal the largest rules differences. Reuse the common steps and name the protocol differences explicitly.

A reasonable later interface is a checked presentation plan containing measured routes, receipt text/amounts, visibility, timing and the precise generic outcomes it covers. This is a proposal, not an implemented API. Keep the card's rules proof and distinct visual accents outside the shared mechanical renderer until repeated cases justify further sharing. Do not build a second rules engine in the animation layer.

No helper extraction is part of this catalogue update. A07 acceptance is frozen in its source90 evidence; product source now contains A08 corrections and the A09 candidate. Reuse does not waive per-identity legal-example, final-state and independent visual acceptance.
